Privacy Policy

ShopSetu · Effective date: 10/09/2026

ShopSetu ("the App", "we", "us") is a B2B ordering application connecting wholesale Distributors and retail Shopkeepers. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the ordering platform.

CategoryWhat we collectWhy
Account credentials Email address (used for one-time-passcode sign-in) To authenticate and secure your account
Profile information Full name, phone number (optional) To identify you within the platform and enable contact between shops and distributors
Shop information Shop name, owner name, phone/email, address, city, area, pincode To register and operate a shop profile, and to fulfill orders
Order data Items ordered, quantities, order status, delivery address, payment status To process, track, and fulfill B2B orders
Product images (Admin/Distributor use) Photos selected from your device gallery or camera To upload and display product catalog images
Uploaded files (Admin use) Spreadsheet (.xlsx) files To bulk-import product catalog data
Local app preferences Session tokens, app settings stored on-device To keep you signed in and remember app preferences

We do not collect precise device GPS location. Address fields (city, area, pincode) are entered manually and are not derived from device location services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and shop profile
  • To process, track, and deliver orders between shopkeepers and distributors
  • To send in-app notifications about order status changes (e.g. confirmed, packed, out for delivery)
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the platform (e.g. blocking abusive accounts)
  • To provide customer support when you contact us

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as follows:

  • Backend infrastructure: App data (accounts, shops, orders, product images) is stored and processed using Supabase, our backend service provider, which hosts our database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Between platform participants: Shop contact and order details are visible to the distributor/admin side of the platform as necessary to fulfill orders. Shopkeepers do not see other shops' data.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the platform and its users.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on Supabase-managed infrastructure and protected using database-level access rules (Row Level Security) so that shopkeepers can only access their own shop and order data. All network communication with our backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

5. Data Retention

We retain account and order data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If your account is deleted, associated order history may be retained in anonymized or aggregated form for record-keeping where historical orders exist.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • You may update your profile and shop information within the App.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us (see below).
  • You may request that your account be deleted; note that orders linked to historical records may be retained where required for accounting/legal purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

ShopSetu is intended for business use by shop owners and distributor staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age.

8. Permissions Used by the App

  • Internet access — required to communicate with our backend servers.
  • Photos / Camera (optional) — used only when uploading a product image; the App does not access your media library beyond the image you explicitly select.
  • File access (Admin only) — used only when selecting a spreadsheet file for bulk product upload.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
